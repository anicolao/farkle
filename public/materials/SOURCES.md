# Material assets

These locally bundled scans are from Poly Haven, licensed CC0.

- `walnut-*.jpg`: [American Walnut Veneer](https://polyhaven.com/a/american_walnut_veneer), 2K diffuse, OpenGL normal, and roughness.
- `cloth-*.jpg`: [Fabric Pattern 07](https://polyhaven.com/a/fabric_pattern_07), 2K OpenGL normal and roughness. Fine-scale surface detail is used with the game's green baize material; the patterned cotton albedo is not used.

[Poly Haven asset license](https://polyhaven.com/license)

Original files were downloaded using metadata from the public Poly Haven API. Material colours, scan scale, normal strength, satin coating, and fibre sheen are adjusted in the scene. The scans were reused from the sibling Cribbage project, retaining their original provenance. The shared Can’t Stop material library generates its lacquer, ivory, felt albedo, maple albedo and roughness, engravings and owner markings locally; the scanned cloth maps supply surface normals and roughness.
